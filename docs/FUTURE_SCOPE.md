# Future Scope & Extensibility Roadmap
**Crime Alert Map (Rakshak AI)**

---

## 1. Advanced Real-Time Integrations

1. **National Emergency Response Support System (ERSS - 112) Integration**: Secure bilateral API integration with state police emergency dispatch centers for automated SOS telemetry push with user consent.
2. **Municipal Smart City IoT Telemetry**: Direct ingestion of live street lighting sensor feeds (SCADA systems) and automated municipal camera uptime monitoring across Tier-1 Smart Cities.
3. **Automotive Telematics & Dashcam Hazard Sharing**: Edge-based pothole, darkspot, and road obstruction detection running locally on connected vehicle dashcams.

---

## 2. Next-Generation AI & Multilingual Expansion

1. **Spatial-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (ST-GNN)**: Advancing beyond tree-based spatial classifiers to Graph Convolutional Networks that model dynamic crime diffusion along connected highway networks.
2. **22 Official Indian Languages Voice Navigation**: Full multilingual voice navigation and Copilot support in Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, and Assamese.
3. **Wearable & Smartwatch Companion App**: Dedicated Apple Watch and Wear OS applications supporting silent haptic heart-rate distress triggers and wrist-based route safety cues.
